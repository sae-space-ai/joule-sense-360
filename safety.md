# Responsible research & compliance
This is a supervised experimental visual-information aid, not approved for independent mobility guidance.
- Never say a route is safe or obstacle-free. Sensors cannot prove absence of hazards.
- No human navigation trials without consent, supervision, safety assessment and appropriate regulatory review.
- Estimates require calibrated depth input and uncertainty; never invent metric distances from monocular RGB.
- User controls memory storage and deletion. Do not identify bystanders or perform face recognition.
- EU review needed for GDPR, MDR potential disability compensation classification, RED/CE for radio equipment, AI Act, cybersecurity/accessibility requirements.
- Claims about production readiness, regulatory class and user benefits must be evidenced.
